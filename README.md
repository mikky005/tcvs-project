The Transparent Conditional Voucher System is a digital subsidy distribution platform developed to improve accountability, transparency, and efficiency in government and NGO palliative programmes across Nigeria.
Rather than distributing physical food items through multiple officials, beneficiaries receive secure digital vouchers redeemable at accredited local vendors.
The platform integrates beneficiary verification using NIN, USSD accessibility, audit logs, public transparency dashboards, and vendor management to ensure that subsidies reach genuine beneficiaries while reducing fraud and corruption
